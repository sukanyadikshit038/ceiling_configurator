# Cloud products — demo shot list and narration

Preset-led, ending on the ceiling. Target **~80 seconds**. Nine beats.

Every label, count and value below was read out of the running app, not
remembered. Where a number depends on the room it says so.

---

## Before you record

| | |
|---|---|
| Room | Office — executive open plan, ceiling **7.5 × 7.0 m @ 3.2 m** |
| Opens on | Clouds, blank brush, empty ceiling, Eye view |
| Ranges loaded | **64** Cloud Series artworks, **275** Designer Textile panels |
| Window | 1440 × 900 or wider. Below ~1100 the sidebars crowd the viewport |

1. Hard reload so the brush is blank and nothing is placed.
2. If anything is on the ceiling: **Clear all → Remove all N?** (it confirms in place).
3. Leave **Place** as the tool. Beat 4 switches to Select.

## The beats

### 1 · Cold open — 0:00–0:07
**On screen** Empty ceiling, Eye view. Left panel reads Tool · Product (Clouds) · Layout presets.
**Do** Nothing. Let it sit two seconds, then click **Spin** for a slow orbit.
**Say** "An acoustic ceiling, configured in the browser. It opens on clouds — and an empty room."

### 2 · One click fills it — 0:07–0:18
**Do** Layout presets → **Hexagon field**. Click **Corner**.
**On screen** 20 hexagons appear at once. The dropdown keeps showing "Hexagon field".
**Say** "Start from a layout. Hexagon field — twenty 1200 mm panels, spaced and centred to the room it's in."

### 3 · Try another — 0:18–0:28
**Do** Layout presets → **Circle drift**. Then **Below**.
**On screen** 23 circles, staggered rows, three different drop heights.
**Say** "Or a different one. Circle drift staggers the rows and hangs them at three heights."

### 4 · Change all of them at once — 0:28–0:44  ← the strongest beat
**Do** **Select** tool → **Box select on** → drag a box over the whole ceiling.
**On screen** Right panel: "23 clouds", then **ALL 23 — SAME PRODUCT · Every field below applies to all 23 at once."
**Do** In that panel, Design → **CL-05**.
**On screen** All 23 repaint together.
**Say** "Select the lot, and every field applies to all of them. Change the artwork once — the whole ceiling follows."

### 5 · Colour — 0:44–0:52
**Do** Same panel, Colour → the red chip.
**Say** "Four colourways per design. The chips are the colour the artwork actually averages to, measured off the image."

### 6 · The fabric range — 0:52–1:06
**Do** Series → **Designer Textiles**.
**On screen** Every shade at once under eight group headings; pick one and five weave thumbnails of that shade appear beneath.
**Say** "Or move to the fabrics. Two hundred and seventy-five panels — fifty-five shades, five weaves. Pick the colour first, then see it in every weave."

### 7 · Height — 1:06–1:14
**Do** Scroll to **Suspension height**, drag 480 → about 900. Type a number to show the box works too.
**On screen** Every panel rises on its wire.
**Say** "Drop height is a slider or a typed number, fifty to two thousand millimetres."

### 8 · One panel on its own — 1:14–1:22  *(optional, drop if running long)*
**Do** Click a single cloud → **Focus this cloud**.
**On screen** That panel alone against the ceiling.
**Say** "And any one of them can be looked at on its own."

### 9 · Close — 1:22–1:32
**Do** Escape out of focus. **Preview** on, **Spin** on, **Corner**.
**Say** "Real models, at real sizes, on a real ceiling."

## Snags worth knowing before you shoot

**The grid overlay cannot be switched off from the UI.** `showGrid` is store
state with no control bound to it, so the beauty shots in beats 3 and 9 keep the
faint grid. It reads as a working drawing rather than a render. Either live with
it, or say the word and I will put a toggle in the Display panel — it is small.

**Preview does not hide the panels.** It changes the render, not the chrome, so
the sidebars stay on screen throughout. If you want a full-bleed final frame
that needs building.

**The preset dropdown stays open until you click away.** It is a menu, so after
choosing a layout the list closes on its own — but if you open it to browse,
click the viewport before the camera move or it sits over the ceiling.

**Beat 4 needs Box select ON.** Without it, dragging orbits the camera instead
of selecting. The panel says so, but on camera it looks like nothing happened.

**Counts move with the room.** 20 / 23 above are for the 7.5 × 7.0 m office. A
different room gives different numbers — re-read them before narrating.
